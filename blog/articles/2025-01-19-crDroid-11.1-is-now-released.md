## crDroid 11.1 is now released!
----------------

We’re thrilled to announce the release of **crDroid 11.1**, based on **Android 15 QPR1**!  
This update represents a major milestone as it includes a **complete rewrite of the codebase**, enabling a more robust and future-proof platform for your devices.  

Due to this extensive overhaul, providing a detailed changelog for this release is challenging. However, rest assured that every aspect of the system has been refined, optimized, and modernized.

### What’s new in crDroid 11.1 ###
**Built on Android 15 QPR1**

- Enjoy the latest features, security patches, and performance improvements introduced in Android 15 QPR1.

**Codebase Rewritten**

- A full system rewrite brings improved maintainability and stability.
- Enhanced performance across the board, reducing memory usage and increasing efficiency.

### Closing thoughts ###
----------------

The release of crDroid 11.1 is just the beginning of a new release cycle for crDroid all based on QPR1 now.  
With the rewrite of the codebase, we’ve laid a foundation for faster updates and more exciting features in future releases.

As always, you can download the latest version from our [downloads](https://crdroid.net/downloads) page.  
We’d love to hear your feedback as you explore this release.  

Thank you for your continued support of crDroid!
