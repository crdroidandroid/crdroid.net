# crDroid 13 is now rolling out

The next major version of crDroid is starting to make its way to supported devices. Based on Android 17, crDroid 13 brings the latest Android platform to users while continuing to build on the customization and features that define the crDroid experience.

Some devices have already received the initial builds, with more devices expected to follow as development and testing progress.

## crDroid 13

crDroid 13 brings Android 17 to supported devices, along with the platform's updated capabilities and improvements. As with every major Android version, device-specific work is needed to adapt the release, validate functionality and deliver a reliable experience across different hardware.

Availability will vary by device. Builds depend on maintainer availability, device support and the status of the required device trees and dependencies, so not every supported device will receive an update at the same time.


Check the crDroid downloads page and your device's official support thread for availability and device-specific installation instructions.  
Before installing, read the instructions for your exact device and make sure you understand any requirements for upgrading from an earlier version.

## Testing and feedback

Community testing is an important part of preparing crDroid 13 for a broader rollout. If you encounter an issue, share useful details such as your device model, build version, steps to reproduce the problem and logs where applicable.

Join the [crDroid Community on Telegram](https://t.me/crDroidAndroid) to discuss the release and share feedback.
