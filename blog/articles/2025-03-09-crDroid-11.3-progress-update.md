## crDroid 11.3 – Merging Android 15 QPR2 will take time

We know many of you are eagerly waiting for crDroid 11.3 based on **Android 15 QPR2**, and we appreciate your patience. The integration process is complex, and we want to ensure that everything runs smoothly. As a result, **it will take some time to merge and fine-tune** before we can deliver a stable release.  
Before the final release, we expect to roll out **a few beta builds** to gather feedback and make necessary improvements. Stay tuned for further announcements!

## Thank You for Your Donations!

We would like to extend our gratitude to the community for the **donations received last month**. Thanks to your support, we have significantly reduced the number of ads across our website.  
If we manage to **maintain the same donation level each month**, we will **completely disable ads for all pages**. For now, we've already **toned down ads a lot**, making the browsing experience much better.  
Your continued support makes a real difference, and we appreciate every contribution!  
If you feel you want to contribute with a donation, visit our [support us](https://crdroid.net/donate) page.
