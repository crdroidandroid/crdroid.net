# crDroid 12.6 – Android 16 QPR2 Is Here 🚀

Yeah yeah... late to post the news on the blog, but here we are...  
crDroid **12.6** is officially rolling out, and this one is a major foundation update rather than just a routine bump.


## 🔧 Based on Android 16 QPR2

crDroid 12.6 is now fully rebased on Android 16 QPR2 code.  
This means:
- New platform-level improvements from Google
- Better system stability and performance
- Updated framework behavior aligned with the latest Android direction

## 🔐 January 2026 security updates

This release includes the **January 2026 Android Security Patch Level**, keeping your device protected against the latest known vulnerabilities.

Security patches are synced directly from upstream AOSP and merged cleanly into the new QPR2 base.

## ⚠️ Important: Signing key change (No OTA)

There is one important caveat with this release:

**Due to a signing key change, OTA updates are not possible for crDroid 12.6.**

### What does this mean?
- You **cannot** update from a previous build via OTA
- The update **requires manual sideloading**

### The good news ✅
- Sideloading is quick and straightforward
- It can be done easily via recovery
- No data wipe is required (unless your recovery/setup is broken)

Once you’re on 12.6, future OTAs will work normally again.
Yes, the lack of OTA is annoying — but it’s unavoidable and temporary.  
Do the sideload once, and you’re back on track.

As always, thanks for using **crDroid** and supporting open-source Android ❤️
