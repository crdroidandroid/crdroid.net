Hey everyone! 👋  

Lately, we’ve noticed an increasing push to **trash crDroid’s name in some toxic ROM communities**.  
While it’s unfortunate to see negativity spreading, we want to make one thing clear—**we are not here to fight back**. This isn’t a competition, a war, or a battle for dominance.  
crDroid exists because we love what we do, not because we want to prove we’re better than anyone else.

**We respect all ROM projects and the work that goes into them**. Open-source development thrives when there’s collaboration, not division.  
Every ROM has its own strengths, and the beauty of custom ROMs is that users have the freedom to choose what works best for them.  
If crDroid isn’t your preference, **that’s completely fine—there are plenty of other great ROMs out there, and we encourage you to use whatever suits you best.**

That being said, **crDroid is not a business, and we’re not in this for profit**. 
This is purely a passion project, built and maintained by people who love Android and want to share something useful with the community. Our main goal has always been to **keep crDroid free, open, and accessible to everyone**.

So while some may try to drag us into unnecessary drama, **we won’t be distracted from what truly matters—developing crDroid, improving the user experience, and continuing to provide a reliable and customizable ROM**.

At the end of the day, **we do this for fun, for passion, and it just happens that we want to share our work with you**. Let’s keep the Android modding scene open, friendly, and focused on what really counts—giving users the best experience possible.
