# Goodbye 2025, Welcome 2026 🎉  
### A Message from the crDroid Team

As 2025 comes to an end, we want to take a moment to look back at what has been one of the most important years in crDroid’s history — and to thank everyone who made it possible.

---

## A Growing Community 🚀

This year, the crDroid community grew more than ever. New users joined us, long-time users stayed, and contributors continued to push the project forward. Seeing so many people trust crDroid as their daily driver is both humbling and motivating. You’re the reason this project keeps moving.

---

## A Big Year for Android Updates 📱

2025 was packed with development work:

- Delivered **a large number of Android 15 updates**, for both older and newer devices  
- Introduced **Android 16 support**, with **QPR1 already merged**, laying solid groundwork for the future  

Keeping devices up to date, stable, and enjoyable to use remains our top priority, and this year highlighted just how dedicated our maintainers truly are.

---

## Challenges, Transparency, and Community Support 💙

Let’s be honest — this year wasn’t easy on the financing side.

We explored different ad systems, evaluated what worked and what didn’t, and ultimately made the decision to **remove ads entirely**. In their place, we introduced a **donation-based system** — and the response from the community was incredible.

Your support proved that crDroid doesn’t need intrusive solutions to survive. It needs people who believe in the project — and you showed up.

---

## Looking Ahead to 2026 🔮

Our focus for the next year is clear:

- Continue delivering updates for **all supported devices**
- Further polish and expand **Android 16 support**
- Prepare for and integrate **upcoming QPR2 changes**, which are already in sight  

No hype, no empty promises — just steady development, clean code, and the crDroid experience you expect.

---

## Thank You ❤️

- To our **users** — thank you for choosing crDroid every day  
- To our **supporters and donors** — you’re keeping the project alive  
- To our **maintainers and contributors** — your time, effort and dedication are the backbone of every release  

crDroid exists because of its community, and 2025 proved that louder than ever.

---

From all of us on the crDroid team,  
**Happy New Year 2026** 🎆  
Here’s to another year of growth, stability, and pushing Android further.

— **The crDroid Team**
