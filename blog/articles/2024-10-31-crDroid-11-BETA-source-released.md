## crDroid 11 BETA source released: Based on Android 15

We are thrilled to announce the release of the crDroid 11 BETA source, marking a significant milestone in our commitment to providing a customizable and performance-driven Android experience.  
This latest iteration is built upon Android 15, bringing the newest features and enhancements to our users.

### Key Highlights of crDroid 11 BETA ###
-   **Foundation on Android 15**: By integrating the latest Android version, crDroid 11 BETA offers users the most recent advancements in security, performance, and user interface design.
-   **Beta Quality Release**: As a beta release, this version is intended for testing and feedback purposes. Users may encounter bugs or stability issues, and we encourage reporting any anomalies to help us refine the final product.

### Device compatibility and upcoming builds ###

We are diligently working to extend support to a wide range of devices. Initial builds for select devices will be available in the coming days starting today, with additional devices to follow soon after. For the latest information on supported devices and build availability, please visit our download page.  

### How to get involved ###

We invite developers and enthusiasts to contribute to the crDroid project.  
By participating in testing and providing feedback, you play a crucial role in enhancing the stability and performance of crDroid 11.  
To get started, access the source code on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/crdroidandroid/android).
