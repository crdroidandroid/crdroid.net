# We Removed All Ads — Your Support Matters More Than Ever

At **crDroid**, we’ve always aimed to provide the best possible experience for our community — both in our ROM and on our website.  
Today, we’ve taken a big step toward making that experience even better: **we’ve removed all ads from our site**.

No more distractions, no more slow page loads caused by ad scripts — just the content you came for.  
This change is meant to make browsing smoother, cleaner, and more enjoyable for everyone.

---

## Relying on Community Support

With ads gone, we no longer have any income from them.  
From now on, **our only source of support will be donations**. This means we’re counting on our amazing community to help keep crDroid alive and growing.

If you value what we do, please consider making a contribution.  
We support donations via **[Donorbox and Patreon](https://crdroid.net/donate)**.  
Every little bit helps — whether it’s the cost of a coffee or more — it all goes directly into maintaining and improving crDroid.
