# crDroid 12.7 – February 2026 Security Patch

crDroid **12.7** is now rolling out, bringing the February 2026 Android security patches along with further refinements on top of the 12.6 release.

This update continues our ongoing focus on polishing the overall experience, tightening things up, and squashing bugs rather than introducing flashy new features.

## What’s new

- Merged February 2026 Android security patches
- General bug fixes reported since 12.6
- Performance and stability improvements
- Additional fine-tuning and under-the-hood cleanup

No major feature drops this time—just steady improvements aimed at making the ROM more reliable and consistent in day-to-day use.

## Notes

As always, device-specific changes may vary depending on the maintainer. If you’re coming from 12.6, this should be a smooth update and is recommended for everyone due to the latest security patches.

If you encounter issues, report them through the usual channels so they can be addressed in upcoming builds.
