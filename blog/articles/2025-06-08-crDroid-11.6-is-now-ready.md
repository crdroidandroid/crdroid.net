## crDroid 11.6 is here!
------------------------

We’re happy to announce the release of **crDroid 11.6**, continuing our mission to deliver a streamlined, reliable, and customizable Android experience. This version rolls out with the **June 2025 security patches** and a collection of system-level fixes, UI adjustments and under-the-hood changes.

> ⚠️ **Note:** Due to recent changes in Google's validation methods, **Play Integrity no longer passes out of the box** on crDroid 11.6. We'll continue exploring viable solutions, but users relying on apps that require strong integrity checks may experience issues.

### What's new in crDroid 11.6 ###

#### Security & Core Updates
- Updated Android security patches to **June 2025**.
- Updated translations for a more polished and localized user experience.

#### System Enhancements
- **Initial crDroid 11.6 release!**
- **PixelPropsUtils**:
  - Fully **removed Google Play Services spoofing** — it was fun while it lasted.
  - **Spoofed Delta Force** game to appear as **ASUS ROG Phone 6D** for compatibility.
- **SystemUI**:
  - Improved **alignment between keyguard clock and date**.
- Fixed issue with **Quiet Mode** toggling not behaving as expected.
- **Bluetooth playback**: Addressed specific scenarios where playback could fail.
- Fixed issue where **global hardware button wake** triggered unintended screen-on behavior.

#### Launcher3 Improvements
- Fixed issue where **cloned apps** wouldn’t show up in the **app drawer**.

#### Miscellaneous
- A number of **small changes and refinements** sprinkled throughout the system.

### Final notes ###
-------------------

**crDroid 11.6** may seem like a small step, but it packs meaningful quality-of-life improvements and important security updates. Whether it's smoother wake behavior, better clock alignment, or app drawer consistency — your experience just got sharper.

Download the latest build from our [downloads page](https://crdroid.net/downloads) and keep enjoying the crDroid experience.

As always, thank you for your continued support. Stay tuned for more!
