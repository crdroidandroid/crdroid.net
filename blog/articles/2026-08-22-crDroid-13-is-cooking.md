# crDroid 13 development update: Now in Alpha

Since it has been a while we've posted anything on the blog, lets bring an update about the development of **crDroid 13** that is officially underway and work is actively progressing towards the next major release.

At the moment, crDroid 13 is in the **ALPHA stage**. Features are being added and refined as development continues, with the team focusing on bringing the new release together while laying the groundwork for a stable and polished experience.

As with any alpha-stage software, things are still changing.

## Current Development

The current focus is on implementing and refining features for crDroid 13. As development progresses, more functionality will be introduced, tested, and polished before the project moves toward the next stage.

The Alpha stage is an important part of the development process, allowing features to mature and issues to be identified and addressed along the way.

## What comes next?

The current goal is to reach the **BETA stage in September**, assuming development continues to progress as planned and there are no major roadblocks.

The transition from Alpha to Beta will depend on the overall stability of the builds, the progress of feature development and how well the current issues are addressed.

For now, the focus remains on development and testing. More features and improvements will continue to land as crDroid 13 evolves.

Stay tuned for further updates as development continues.
